DEEP CUT

Five questions a day. Name a real player who fits.
The deeper the cut, the more points the scout gives you.

How scoring works

  • Each answer is checked by Claude, the scout. Wrong or skipped answers score 0.
  • The scout rates every correct answer from 1 to 10 on how deep a cut it is — worth 10 to 100 points. A superstar scores low; a 12-game call-up scores high.
  • Misspelled a name? Before you lock in, the scout suggests the right spelling.
  • Your daily total (max 500) sets your rung for the day.
  • You can't see anyone's answers until you lock in all five. Honor system: no looking things up.
